## 2. Shadow does not steer: keep it as it is

Steering in shadow would change the levels a child is served for the sake of evidence nobody yet uses, and unsteered sightings are the cleaner measurement: they are not selected by our own rule. Slow is acceptable while no child plays. Reverse: once real sittings exist, a mode with fewer than 30 shadow namings after a month of play gets steering in shadow for that mode.

## 3. S2's count: both kinds count, each for itself
