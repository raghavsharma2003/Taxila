2. **`simpler`s that are the line before in other words** are rewritten to come at the idea from another side (a different object, a smaller case, a count the child can do). 3. **The `did` clauses and frames.** Every `did` reads as a thing done, in the order the child did it, and is easy to say. Named: c4 s3 c3 (it lists 1/4, 1/10, 1/7 in the card's order while saying smallest to largest); c5-t01 s3 c2 ("11 lambe aur 11 chhote gap ki doori milaakar dekhi"); c5-t02 s3 c3 ("ki … ki … ka size"); c6 s3 c2 ("naapa": she worked it out, she did not measure). New `joints.afterKeyGoOn`: three forms for a child who still says "pata nahi" after an answer was worked (it says they will meet this again and goes on; no blame, no praise). Two more forms each for `asidePark` and `afterTryFirst` if a script has fewer than three.
