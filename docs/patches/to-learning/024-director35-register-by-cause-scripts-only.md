## Part 2: the causes, one at a time

Register has been 3.5 of 5 in every topic for nine runs. The judges' 180 register rows of director35 sort into: the length or shape of
a turn 65, script sentences and reasons 35, help 34, her closes and other 28, fixed forms 18. Three of these have a part that lives in
the scripts. Each is changed on two topics first (c6-maths-ch06-t01, live; c4-maths-ch06-t01, staged), walked offline, run with the
model (eight lessons) and read by two judges against the same topics' director35 reports, before the next cause is touched. A cause is
kept when both judges' rows for it fall by half on the two topics and no axis falls; then it is rolled out to the other seven.

- **Cause 1, the first teaching turn.** A child who has said "pata nahi" twice at the greeting hears 63 to 66 words (five reports). Cut
  the hook to one sentence that starts the lesson, or the point to one example: never the card's givens, never the board's own line.
  Rule 3 of Part 1 is the measure.
- **Cause 2, sentences and reasons as written.** Each judge's list under C and under "Register rows by cause" (b) and (c): a reason that
  only restates the answer, a short reason that leaves out the why after two misses, a frame said twice in a skill or once in every
  worked close ("Toh poora jawab:"), two words for one thing, a sentence no teacher would say. Rewrite the sentence; do not add one.
- **Cause 3, what help has to say.** (a) The bridges' easier wordings (decision item 4): each says in plain words what was just learnt
  and what comes next from it; none is the bridge with words taken out ("Ab metre ki baari hai." is not help). (b) A hint that repeats
  the reason of the turn before, or all but states its answer (c5-ch02-t01 s1's second follow-up card), or meets a count mistake with
  a wording about size. (c) The four second pictures (item 5): c6-maths-ch06-t01 s1's worked step 1, c4-maths-ch06-t01 s1 e3,
  c6-maths-ch07-t01 s1's first follow-up card, c5-maths-ch02-t03 s1 e2: a `pictureBoard` and `pictureCaption` that draw the same thing
  another way and do not settle the card.

## How

Authors work one script each, with the script checker green in every mode before and after; every changed card is solved again from
its words and its board. A reader who has not seen the script reads it cold on six passes: the five of 023, and a sixth: each own step
and each follow-up card against the board it is posed on (what does the sentence point at, and is it drawn at that moment). Main
writes no script sentence in this round.
