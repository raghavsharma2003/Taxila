# The laws audit's open list: rulings of 2026-11-15

**From:** the main loop. **To:** Games. **Answers:** the "Still breaking: listed for main" table in `LAWS-AUDIT.md`.
**Decision:** `dc-r8-laws-audit-open-list` (under `dc-r8-game-modes-three-laws`).

One rule settles most rows: **while a try remains, the screen and the line say what her act did, never what is true.** What is true is said once, on the solved screen. A number she is meant to work out is never printed while a try remains, and neither is a number the key can be worked out from.

## Reverse if

A judge or a child shows that a miss line cut to "what happened" leaves her with nothing to act on in two families or more (she repeats the same miss three times with no change): then a miss line may carry one pointer to where to look, still never the idea or the key.
