Every painted image was made on Azure with gpt-image-2 (deployment taxila-image): 37 images out of the 90-image cap, about USD 8 by token count. Option 3 is drawn in code and needed no images. Each family's five expression frames are edits of its own front, so the identity comes from the pixels, not from a description. Every prompt, size, date and token count is in gen.json.

How each option was judged

  • My own eye, at full size and at the real slot sizes: 90 × 117 and 250 px tall in the lesson, 150 × 190 in the home window, 38 × 48 in the game. I picked each family's front by eye before any judge result came back, so the judges could not steer the pick.
  • A blind check by two vision models on Azure from different makers: gpt-5.6-sol (n=3 per front) and Kimi K2.6 (n=2 per front). Neither was told her age, her job, her country or the product. Open questions came first (age, job, where she is from), then the yes/no ones. As a control, today's chibi was read as childish in 5/5 runs, and none of the four options was. So the judges can see childishness when it is there.
  • An identity check: each family's six frames as a grid, judged by the same two models (n=5 per family). It asks whether all six are the same person, and what each face is doing.
  • Skin against Monk 6, Asha's band in TEACHER-VISUAL, measured as g9.mjs does: the per-channel median of four 25 px patches (cheeks and forehead), averaged in linear light and converted to L*a*b*. The g9 bar is ΔL* ≤ 3 and ΔC* ≤ 4.

What the judges caught, and what I changed

  • The first thinking frames (a side glance) read as a sceptical side-eye in 3/3 runs for the painted and the lamplight options. That breaks the rule that her face never reacts to an answer. I redid them with the eyes up and the brows level. Now 1 in 3 runs still calls the upward glance a mild eye-roll. In motion that is a timing problem: the glance should last under 3.5 s.
  • The first speaking frames read as surprised (raised brows, wide eyes). I redid them with relaxed brows. Now every run reads "speaking".
  • The line-of-light listening frame is still flagged as a sceptical glance in 2/3 gpt-5.6-sol runs, even after one fix. It is the weakest frame on the page.

Skin: every painted option is too orange for Monk 6

As generated, all three painted fronts are lighter than the Monk 6 swatch (ΔL* +5 to +14) and about twice as saturated (ΔC* +21 to +32). So is today's chibi. When the skin is graded to hit the bar exactly, it turns ashy and grey on the cream lamplight (right). A half-way grade (middle, C* about 37) looks the most like real skin. The g9 bar was written for a 3D material under a stage light, not for painted pixels, so the 2D target needs a decision: grade to the swatch, or measure inside the window's lamplight.

What this cannot tell you

  • A model's opinion is a stand-in, not yours, and not a child's or a parent's. No child or parent has seen these.
  • These are stills. None of them has been rigged, so how each one moves is a forecast (see RIG-NOTES.md), not something I watched.
  • "Reads as Indian" leans heavily on the saree and bindi. The judges said so themselves.
  • The judges see "teacher" in all four. That is the clothes and the framing doing the work, so it does not separate the options.
