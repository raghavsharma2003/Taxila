## V1 · Knowing whether the child learnt it, and moving on accordingly
**The breakthrough:** "learnt" means the child can still do it later, in a new form, without help. It does not mean they answered once.
Every move she makes is chosen from a calibrated estimate of that, and the parent sees the evidence.

**100% =**
1. **Grading truth:**
   - 0 wrong grades across ≥ 2,000 randomised correct, incorrect and partial answers on every module and item form (owner-truth patches 01-04 + RS-4 `gradeAnswer`);
   - frame claims are never trusted;
   - multi-part keys are graded partial.
2. **Placement:**
   - every new child is placed by the adaptive test (RS-6);
   - 0 first questions ≥ 2 classes below the child's level on the 385 class 4-7 topics (two raters, κ reported);
   - a "harder" path exists for every topic and the child can ask for it.
3. **Mastery means retention:** a skill is "secure" only after a correct delayed check (≥ 2 days) in a new form, without hints. The
   learner model is calibrated (ECE ≤ 0.05 on held-out traces) and never shows false mastery above 2% in simulation.
4. **Moving on:** advance, consolidate and reteach are chosen by policy from that estimate. The two failure directions are measured:
   - boredom: too easy, the child is not challenged for 3 or more items in a row;
   - overload: wheel-spinning, 10 tries without 3 right in a row.
   Each must stay ≤ 5% of skill-sessions.
5. **Evidence:** every "learnt" claim to a parent links to the exact moments (answers, delayed checks) behind it, and the claims audit stays green.
6. **Proof on children:** on the pilot, delayed-check accuracy on skills marked "secure" is ≥ 85%, and the predicted P(correct) is
   calibrated against real delayed outcomes.

## V2 · Understanding learning behaviour automatically
**The breakthrough:** reading the child's knowledge state from words, behaviour and voice timing, compared with their own baseline,
named by the action it licenses. It never claims an emotion (Microsoft Code of Conduct restriction 12; on-device / self-hosted per the
owner's AWS/Neon directive).

**100% =**
1. Text and behaviour signals (`server/signals`) are read by the Director, comprehension, relational and pace consumers every turn, with all 14 integration steps done. They have been on in shadow and then live.
2. Voice-timing knowledge states (`voicesig`) run on device with the shared front-end (one tap for duplex and voice signals), with per-child baselines in Neon behind the parent toggle.
3. Each state (fragile-correct, confident-wrong, searching vs not-known, guessing, stuck productive vs unproductive, disengaging)
   ships with its measured precision and recall. A state goes live only at **precision ≥ 0.80 on children** (the pilot). Below that it stays
   shadow and says so on the status page.
4. **Added value proven:** with signals on, delayed-check outcomes predicted better than text-and-task alone (Δ AUROC with CI), on the pilot.
5. 0 safety-turn leaks, and no emotion words in any output (lint plus 10k-turn test).

## V3 · World-class content on the go, asked and unasked
**The breakthrough:** a duplex for content. Several candidates (games, simulations, cinematic explainers, whiteboard, images) are built in
parallel while the talk goes on. They are re-ranked and invalidated as it changes, and the right one is shown at the right moment, never stale, broken or loading.

**100% =**
1. **Coverage:** every one of the 385 class 4-7 topics has at least one real-time game or simulation **and** one cinematic explainer
   (engine spec-backed, NCERT-tagged, key-graded), plus whiteboard plans for every explanation beat.
2. **Quality bar:**
   - every engine is ≥ 4/5 on the STUDIO-V2 rubric from two judges plus a human spot check;
   - it runs at 60 fps on the reference ₹10k phone (adaptive resolution allowed, reported per engine);
   - nothing in the catalogue is a quiz in costume.
3. **Speculative stagecraft live:**
   - right-artifact-ready-when-needed ≥ 90%;
   - stale or wrong reveals = 0;
   - visible failures = 0 over the EXP-30 battery plus 200 simulated lessons;
   - a child's request is answered on stage within 3 s p90;
   - generation frequency of ≥ 1 visual every 3 minutes of teaching on average, unless the child is in flow on a task.
4. Live generation for anything outside the catalogue passes validators before reveal, and the teacher draws on the board instead whenever it is not ready.
5. Cost per lesson-hour is within the MODEL-STACK budget, reported.

## V4 · Human-like teacher
**100% =**
1. **Voice:** Diya with the Devanagari step on every Hindi word. Pace and phrasing are tuned by ear on the long-passage page, and blind
   raters (owner + friend) score her ≥ 4/5 on "a real teacher talking to me" in the live product, not in renders.
2. **Face:** the 2D style-C puppet is live in the lesson tile (ships at 4.0, owner 2026-10-05). It has:
   - lip sync to Diya;
   - listening nods timed by the duplex engine;
   - a thinking glance and verdict-neutral expressions.
   Polishing continues to ≥ 4.5 on the judge panel.
3. **Timing:** first audible sound after a finished answer p50 ≤ 900 ms on the India lane, and no talking over the child (V5).
4. **Words:** spoken register, not read text. 0 sentence-shaped recitations from prompts, and the persona invariants are green.
