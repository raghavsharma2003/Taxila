## Decision: dc-r7-script-walk-v6-2 (the board always draws; turns stop stacking)

The teaching layer has passed for five runs in a row (97% taught, 99% verdicts, 0 misgrades), and her words now cause only 5 contradictions. **The reversal is not taken.** The work left is the screen and turn shape.

**Drawing (Studio, with Brain):**
- **G1.** A failing first page never fails the turn. Keep the answered card's board for that segment (Studio). Together with Brain's join of short first pages (1f2c46de), this removes the `unpaged` class.
- **G2.** W5 and W8 accept a close written in the closed card's authored why, on compare@1 and fraction-bars.
- **G3.** Confirm-reveal fires on a correct verdict for the answered card, whether or not her page says the digits. Brain's verdict segment carries the key as the reveal.
- **G4.** A second view exists for every board template a card can use: wholes@1, measured@1 (sides labelled, or the boundary unrolled onto a ruler), shape@1, a single-denominator line, and fraction-bars (as a line). It is always built from the **live card's** figure.
- **G5.** "screen par dikhao (na)" is a picture request.

**Turns (Brain): split policy B is kept, with limits.**
- **T1.** At most one close per turn. A card left more than one turn ago is closed in its own short turn, or not at all.
- **T2.** Each why is said once.
- **T3.** A turn ends after a bridge. The new skill's first point comes next turn.

**Runtime (Brain):**
- **H1.** A hint after a wrong answer is authored: the card's `simpler`, then the remediation say. It is never model-written and never gives the live answer.
- **H2.** An aside is returned to at the promised point, never earlier. The return is one line about what the child actually said, never a repeated canned fact.
- **H3.** Acknowledgements are code's when hers misread the child.
- **H4.** No doubled sentences when Hindi switches in, and no English leftovers.
- **H5.** Every keyed answer gets a verdict.

**Data (Learning, no format change):**
- `simpler` on remediations;
- an answerBoard on every ask whose answer state is drawable (c4 skill 2 first);
- whys that name their value;
- c5-t02 c1/c2 as fraction-bars;
- c5-t01 e2 drawn with its second line;
- a distinct e3 key for c6.

**Harness:** the checker reads fraction-bars and wholes, and keeps the hand-calibrated classes (words / drawing / data).
