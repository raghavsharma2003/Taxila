3. **Round d37 changes the walk only, and is spent on the turn's shape and on what the child hears around an answer, one cause at a time, each measured on two topics before the next** (`dc-r8-one-author-rounds` kept). In this order: (a) a turn never runs an aside's reply or a park into a new point and its card, and the first teaching turn after two give-ups is two turns, the hook and the point's first sentence, then the card (clause 64); (b) a wait says the question (director35 item 3, unchanged); (c) keys and reasons as said (director35 item 8, unchanged), with help at a child's own step taken from the step, and a card's help said over the card's board; (d) a return after a verdict has its joint, a park is always returned to, the goodbye's reply is chosen from what the child said. *Reverses, each on its own pair run:* (a) if two judges hear the split first turn as slow for a child who was ready, the split is kept only after two give-ups at the greeting; (b) and (c) as director35 states; (d) if the joint is heard as one more fixed form, it is dropped and the return waits one turn.
4. **How a change is checked before a run changes (clauses 68 and 69).** After the last fix of a round, an independent reader reads the offline walk of all four children row by row for every script or walk path that changed: the lesson as heard, each sentence against the board the walk put under it. A fix made after that reading is read the same way or does not ship. A finding that a sentence is said over a board it is untrue of is the script's to cure whenever a sentence can cure it, whoever chose the board. Main rules on findings by fixing or removing, and records any finding it sets aside in the judges' prompt, so that the judge of that topic is asked about it by name. *Reverses if* a lesson fails on a row that this reading had in front of it and passed: then a full run is preceded by a two-topic paid run of every changed script, read by two judges, as the three causes were.
5. **The path around a safety turn is the owner's.** What the walk does after a safety turn (it should ask the open card again, not grade the child's next words against it, and no model should draft a teaching turn) is beside frozen code. It is written up for the owner with this run's rows and built only on the owner's yes. The safety turn firing on "yes, they are" is the case for `OWNER.md` row 8.
6. **Learning 025** is the script list of mechanism 10, for the script round after d37. No script changes in d37.
7. **The harness** (evals only, any time): ruling 3's predicate; the meter's mixed number; the two labels; the run's README states safety turns from a search of every teacher row for the helpline numbers.

**What would reverse the whole:** a lesson that passed failing again on something a round introduced, with item 4's reading in place. Then a round is too large to check: a full run reads at most three changed scripts or one changed walk mechanism.
