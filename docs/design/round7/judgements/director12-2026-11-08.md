## Decision: dc-r7-script-walk-v6-4 (no orphan lines; every card ends answered; asides answered)

The reversal is not taken. The v6.3 clause needs P1 and S1–S3 *in place* with more than 10 paging plus second-view contradictions.
- **The count:** paging 14 plus second view 6 is 20.
- **P1 and S2 are only partly in place,** and 12 of the 20 come from two new, named mechanisms: the fragment cut and ask-board paging.
- **Three of the four topics fell from 30 to 9.**

v6.4 closes those mechanisms structurally, and turns the remaining listening and promise faults into predicates.

**Brain:**
- **P2. No orphan lines.**
  - A part whose page is refused is dropped whole, never said over another board.
  - Sentence dedupe drops whole sentences only, never fragments; a remediation is said whole or the walk moves to the next rung.
  - A beat's simpler or recap is drawn on that beat's board.
  - A single-part turn draws its own line's board.
  - Predicate: 0 spoken parts with no page of their own.
- **K1. Every card ends answered.**
  - After a misconception remediation and its then-card close, the original card is posed again, once.
  - A second miss gets the give-up close: its key and why, on its answerBoard.
  - Predicate: every card posed in a lesson ends with its key said. It is answered right by the child, or given up with key and why.
- **Q1. Non-answer turns re-pose only the card,** for try-first, "sawaal kya hai", "phir se bolo" and unclear replies. There is no recital. "Yaad karo" is never said on a live card.
- **H2″. Asides.**
  - Return at the first boundary after the promised card closes, never later.
  - The return is one line that answers what the child said, a question included ("Haan, mujhe bhi Virat ki batting pasand hai!"), and reuses one of their content words.
  - It is model-written under that predicate: up to 20 words, no lesson numbers. Asides are conversation, not teaching.
  - It makes no new promise and uses no fixed template.
  - An aside never returned by the close is returned to at the wrap.
- **T1″. One close per worked card** (no "…kyunki" plus "Toh poora jawab").
- **L1. A Hindi request switches her own lines to Hindi** for the rest of the beat. Root-cause why c4 serves it while c5 and c6 don't.
- **R3.**
  - Acknowledgements are neutral ("Achha.", "Theek hai.") unless the classifier is confident.
  - No word-for-word re-pose; re-pose with the card's own simpler lead-in or a fresh "Phir se dekho:" frame.
- **Harness:**
  - The child's question regex accepts imperatives (lagao / likho / batao / karo).
  - The picture checker reads the second view on screen.
  - The proxies report dropped pages.

**Studio:**
- **S4.** Real second views where none exist:
  - a fraction-parts bar becomes a circle (and the reverse);
  - count-key cards get the uncut whole beside the parts;
  - an unmarked ask line gets bars.
- **S5.** The bring-back trigger words come from the card's own text (story nouns such as sadak and khet) and cover measured@1 and shape@1.
- **S6.**
  - A script board's preselect is gated on her real line, not on its facts row.
  - Catalogue pages are rescaled so they can join.

**Learning (data):**
- **D6.** `figureOf` distinguishes bars from a line. The D2 validator also covers prompts: "patti" or "bar" needs bars on the posing board; "rekha", "line", "nishaan" or "ringed" needs a line, with the mark when one is named.
- **D7.**
  - c5-t02 s1 e1/e2 are posed on unmarked bars.
  - c3 is posed on a single-denominator line with its ring.
  - The marble and pencil answerBoards get their equal companion.
