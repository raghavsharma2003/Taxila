## Decision: dc-r8-director29 (the picture is drawn from the part it is of; a step's easier wording is a rung; the lesson starts after a second request at the greeting; the child's own try is an ending)

1. **A picture turn draws the board the walk named, and the predicate reads what was drawn.** One function decides the board a picture is of (the part being helped, or the answered card's answer board after a key) and Studio's held panel draws that board, never the lesson's held unit by default. The caption is that board's authored caption; where the script has none the turn redraws the board with no second view and no line about a picture. Gated predicate: on a picture turn the held panel's drawn facts are the named board's. It is pinned non-zero on the four steering rows.
2. **The page's start comes from one function on every path**, the easier-words line included: that line is a lead and owns no page.
3. **The picker's ladder gains two rungs.** At an asked worked step a give-up or a clarify gets the step's `simpler` before the step is told. A remediation's unsaid wordings (`sayAlt`, `simpler`) are help for its follow-up card before that card's key. A key is never given in answer to an aside or to a yes.
4. **A second request at the greeting starts the lesson.** She says one authored start line (six words at most) and goes into the hook, the first point and its card. The "nothing easier" line is never said before something has been taught.
5. **After a right answer the short reason is allowed whatever came before it**, a hint included. A worked example's close says the answer without its why when the why and the next card would not fit.
6. **The allowed endings become five:** a card's question, an asked worked step, the child's own step, a check-in after help, a key or a bridge, and "go on, you try" at a card. An aside reply's turn never ends on a check-in on any path (the three rows are closed). A park line is followed by its authored joint, or the park forms are rewritten to lead back themselves.
7. **The short ask is said only when the card's question was said in the turn before.** After a hint about another figure she says the card's authored re-ask.
8. **The record:** `whyShort` only where the short reason was said; a told step and the greeting's question are not `point`; one `start` seam a turn.
9. **The scripts' list** (Learning 018): every caption read against the view that is drawn for its board, and against its worked example's asked steps in words as well as digits, with the checker doing both; an answer caption for every follow-up card; remediations written to be said (a remediation that cannot be said in full sentences inside the limit stands alone and ends on its check-in: said beats fitted); the lines in mechanism 11.

**What is not changed:** the 75-word turn and the 60-word remediation turn; the short reason; the worked examples' shape; the boards; the gate and its strict reading; the stop protocol's check-in (`dc-r8-stop-checkin-stays`); the whiteboard's op cap (`dc-r8-op-cap-stays`: three rows were marked `noFit` in this run, none in a steady lesson); the aside's return as a turn of its own.

**Work orders:** `docs/patches/to-brain/045-director29-picture-rungs-start.md` (items 1 to 8) and `docs/patches/to-learning/018-director29-captions-and-said-lines.md` (item 9, the start line, the worked close).

**The staged 30 stay held.** They additionally need what item 9 gives the pilots: captions checked against the drawn view, and remediations written to be said.

## What would reverse this

- Item 1: a picture turn in director30 whose drawn board is not the one named, on any path → the picture is taken out of the held-board path and drawn as a turn of its own.
- Item 3: two judges finding the step's easier wording before the told step slows a child who has given up → the step is told at the first give-up and the easier wording becomes the hint for a wrong answer only.
- Item 4: two judges finding the start line brushes the child's "aap batao" aside → the greeting's turn itself is rewritten (it asks nothing a child can fail).
- Item 6: two judges finding "go on, you try" alone a dead turn → the card's question is said again after it.
- Item 9: a remediation left to stand alone in more than two places a steady lesson → the 60-word limit for that turn is re-read against the judges' ear, not the scripts cut again.
- The 75-word turn: two judges in director30 finding the verdict-point-card turns too much in one breath → the limit goes to 65 words, as clause 2 said.
- Two topics passing every line with every axis at 3.5 → the staged scripts get the same read three at a time, each judged before the next three.
