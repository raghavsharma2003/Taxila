## Decision: dc-r7-script-walk-v6-1 (run-path drawing, authored "simpler", no model-written teaching)

The teaching and grading layer passes for the fourth run in a row: 96% taught, 99% verdicts, 0 misgrades. Contradictions fell from 73 to 41, and four-fifths of what remains is drawing and data, not her words. **The reversal is not taken.**

**Run path** (Studio with Brain, owned jointly):
- **P1.** Replay the exact director9 rows (c5-t01 steady T21 for confirm-reveal; c5-t02 stuck T18 for the picture) through the real turn path. Find the side that drops each one, fix it, and pin both with run-path replay tests. The unit tests passed while the run failed.
- **P2.** Every picture request draws something new:
  - the card's own figure when it isn't up;
  - otherwise a second view, which now also covers compare@1 and shade-grid@1 (bars, or the item's own figure);
  - the line names what was drawn, written by code from the take's params.

**Runtime** (Brain):
- **R7. No model-written teaching in script mode.**
  - A re-explanation uses the beat's authored `simpler` (below).
  - A right-answer reason uses the authored why, else none.
  - Any model sentence that states a rule or a number is checked against the key and wrongIfMis, and dropped if it states the misconception.
- **R8. Requests are moves.**
  - "samajh nahi aaya" / "pata nahi" → the authored `simpler`. Never the next step, a repeat, or something longer.
  - Try-first, "kis cheez" and dodged repairs never give the live card's answer.
  - A probe answer gets an acknowledgement sentence on the old board, with no next question in that slot.
- **R9. Check-ins.** At most one per beat, and only after new teaching. A non-yes answer is acted on (→ `simpler`), never ignored.
- **R10. Parks and language mode, again.**
  - An aside is parked and returned to (R5 is still unmet).
  - The language mode also switches the card to its authored Hindi form.
- **R11. Rendering.** No echo of the child's English. "Pichhle sawaal" only for a closed card. Step labels match their step.

**Data, v2.3: an additive format change** (Learning; Brain updates SCRIPT_VERSIONS):
- **`simpler` {hi, en} on every explain beat, worked step and card.** One smaller re-explanation that a class-4 child could follow, written from that item's own givens, plus a one-step hint for cards.
- **Every prompt's shape word matches its board's `facts.shape`,** with a check.mjs rule. c6's L-shape card is redrawn or reworded.
- c5-t02's s1 worked board becomes two bars (6 and 3 parts).
- c4's e3 gets an answer-state board, revealed on confirm, and e2/e4 draw their pizzas.
- The 45-word remediation cap (06fe4cb17) carries over.

**Harness:**
- The child treats an imperative card as posed, and caps requests at 2 in a row per card (Learning; sent).
- The checker models confirm-reveal (a "?" over a value her segment states or confirms) and picture draws (nothing new drawn).
