## Decision: dc-r7-script-walk-v6-5 (a picture turn is its own page; frames that read like a person)

**Brain:**
- **P3.** A picture-request turn draws the second view with its own one line only. The card is re-posed on its own board, as a second page, or on the next turn. It is never in the same breath over the alt view.
- **P2′.** When a card's own page is refused, she says no line about that card's figure. The turn drops the card part and logs it (and Learning titles the board).
- **F1, frames.**
  - Every code frame has 3–5 natural Hinglish forms, and none repeats within 4 turns.
  - "Is sawaal ka jawab" for the card just answered or given up; "Pichhle sawaal" only for a card closed earlier.
  - Delete "Theek, yeh tumhari soch thi" and "Achha, tumne apna tareeka bataya".
  - No "Wah", praise or aside return in the same part as a wrong verdict; the return opens its own part.
  - Never invent the child's method. If the read isn't confident, say only the neutral acknowledgement.
  - Never emit fragments or stray tokens ("Sana sahi.", "Aamma.", "Sahi—,").
- **F2, persona.** She makes no first-person claims of taste or experience ("mujhe Virat pasand hai", "maine match dekha"). Asked directly, she answers honestly in one line and turns it to the child: "Main AI teacher hoon, mera koi favourite nahi — par tumhe Virat ki kaunsi baat sabse achhi lagti hai?". It's warm, consistent and never denies being an AI.
- **B1, no beatless model turns.**
  - Every model-written line is anchored to the live beat.
  - A Hindi or clarify request re-says the live beat's own simpler, in Hindi, on its own board. There is no free reteach and no question without a card.
  - She never poses a question that has no card.
- **K2.**
  - "pata nahi" gets the card's simpler; the remediation is kept for a misconception.
  - A remediation already said in the lesson is not re-said: the next rung is the give-up with key and why.
  - The then-card's second miss gives the key.
  - A simpler on a live card re-poses in the same turn.
  - "aage chalo" or "woh ho gaya" moves on.
  - No model "break".
- **A1.** Canned facts are filtered from her drafts on every turn, not only on returns. An aside unreturned by its card's close returns at the next boundary, and at the latest at break or stop.
- **Speed.** Measure where the p50 went (2.64 → about 3.06 s). If the H2″ retry call is the cause, run it only when her draft lacks the return line, with a tight timeout.
- **Harness.**
  - The picture checker gets `unrolled`.
  - A spoken line whose page was dropped counts as a contradiction.
  - The goodbye waits for an open card's close when there is one.

**Studio:**
- **S7.** A second view is built from the last page on screen.
