## What fails now, by mechanism

1. **How a turn is assembled (register; all four judges, independently).** Joints are no longer what holds register down. What does: a verdict, its why, the next point and its card in one breath of 60 to 80 words (69 of 671 turns are over 60 words); the aside's return question glued onto a verdict or a key; one fixed form in every lesson for the Hindi switch, the park, try-first and "Phir se dekho:"; "Dekho," as the why's opener (11 times in c4 steady); a filler doubled across a joint; a wrap that is a recap with no word to the child.
2. **Help has a two-turn memory.** A Hindi request gets the point word for word (c4 stuck 10, offtopic 32; c5-t01 offtopic 34); a hint or a third help re-says the point (c4 steady 62, stuck 12; c5-t02 stuck 12, steering 32); help on a worked problem is its `simpler`, which paraphrases the line just said (c6 steady 26, 60, stuck 42, offtopic 38; c5-t02 stuck 44); a give-up on a card gets the point's help (c4 steering 32, the same row as director23; c5-t01 stuck 6).
3. **A part said twice or not at all.** c5-t02 steering 38: the e3 point is said again one turn after it was said, over the worked example's board (the contradiction). c4 steady 96: the e2 point is never said and its card arrives cold.
4. **The sentence about the child** (above): absent in 2 of 4.
5. **The `seams` record is wrong about kinds.** Every joint it records was said as recorded. But the hook is recorded as a verdict, a check-in as an aside, the closing miss verdict as a point, the Hindi switch not at all, and a miss verdict is recorded on rows where no miss word was said. Predicates that read the record cannot see a stacked joint at a Hindi switch.
6. **Smaller.** A second or third aside gets a flat reply; a third AI line asks again what the child answered at the first (c5-t01 offtopic 28). After "bye" she offers a menu and once names the buttons aloud. "Hindi mein bolti hoon" is followed by Hinglish. The harness still raises false `contradictionsV6` and false-key flags.
7. **Script.** Three points whose `simpler` is written for the card that follows (c5-t02 S1 e1, S2 e3, S3 e3); worked-example `simpler`s that paraphrase; two captions not heard in this run ("0 se two tak"; c5-t01 s1 e2's caption describes a strip its board does not have); "upar" for both the top strip and the numerator; a key slot said raw ("sahi jawab: sonu ka").

## Decision: dc-r8-director24 (the model writes the joins; a turn carries one new thing; help remembers the lesson)

Supersedes `dc-r8-director23`. The seam pass's table, the reader's record, the captions, the boards and everything listed under "what stays" stand.

**M, the model writes the joins (Brain; clause 1).** The seam pass keeps deciding where a join goes and what kind it is. What is said there is written by the reply model, in the call the turn already makes: it is given the turn's parts in order with their kinds, and returns one short join for each seam the table marks and one opener for each verdict and each why. Authored parts are said word for word, as now. Code checks every join before it is said: at most 12 words; no digit, fraction word or script term from a card not yet answered; no question; no word from the screen or the app; not the same as any join in her last four turns. A join that fails, or a call that is late, falls back to the table's authored form, and the row records which was said. The prompt gives the shape of a join for each kind, never a line (a sentence in a prompt gets recited). First the record is made right: every part's kind is what it is (hook, check-in, miss verdict, Hindi switch), and a miss verdict is recorded only where a miss word is said.

**L, a turn carries one new thing (Brain).** A turn that would run over 60 words breaks at a check-in: a verdict with its why and the next point end on the point's check-in, and the card comes on the next turn. The aside's return question is a turn of its own, in one of the script's `asideReturn` forms, never glued onto a verdict or a key. The wrap is two turns when the child is still there: the "covered" sentences, then the sentence to her and the goodbye.

**H3, help remembers the lesson (Brain, Learning; clause 6).** Help of any kind never says a sentence she has said anywhere in the lesson. The first help on a part is that part's own unsaid wording (a point's second wording or simpler; a card's hint; a worked problem's second telling, never its `simpler` when that paraphrases). A second request for help of any kind on the same part goes to the worked answer with the board, as the clause says. A Hindi request is not a request for help and does not count as one (amended the same day, on Brain's question: sending it to the worked answer would take the question away from a child who asked only for the language): it gets the part's unsaid wording in Hindi words, or, with none left, the same part once more in Hindi words and then the question; a second Hindi request on the same part is a second request and goes to the worked answer. A give-up on a card gets that card's own hint, never the point's. Learning rewrites the three point `simpler`s that are written for the card and the worked-example `simpler`s the judges list.

**T3, the sentence about the child (Brain, Learning; clause 4).** Code picks the fact: the hardest card she answered right at the first try, by the script's own order within the last skill she reached, and never the closing question. Learning authors for each check card a short clause that says what doing it was (`did`, in the second person: "1/3 ke neeche do 1/6 ke tukde gine"). The wrap says "Aaj tumne {did}." in one of three authored frames. No card right at the first try: no sentence, and the goodbye stands alone.

**P, a part is said once (Brain).** A point, a worked step or a card's first telling is said exactly once unless the child asks for it again: never dropped (c4 steady 96), never said again because of a reply (c5-t02 steering 38). A gated predicate counts both from the row's parts.

**K3, A3 (Brain).** After a wrong answer to a yes-or-no card: "Nahi," and then the one sentence that states the fact. A second or third aside gets a reply in the child's words; an AI line never asks again what she has already answered. After "bye" she says goodbye; the stop menu is for "ruk jao", not for a goodbye. A Hindi request is promised in words that are true of what follows ("aasaan shabdon mein").

**Harness (Brain; asked for the third time).** The false `contradictionsV6` flags on a verdict over its own answer board and the false-key flags the judges list are removed or corrected before the next run.

**Learning.** The `did` clauses; the `simpler`s above; the two captions; "upar"; the raw key slots; c5-t02's S3 `covered` sentence.

**What stays as it is.** The order laws. The seam table and its record. The reader's record. Pictures always draw, with authored captions and the question alone. The answer board at the first word of the key. Bridges, try-first, re-asks. Grading against verified keys. The safety code.

**Scale gate after director24:** met by c6. Not met by c4 and c5-t01 (register only) or c5-t02 (register and one contradiction). The staged 30 stay held from children.

**director25 runs when** M, L, H3, T3 and P are in with their predicates at 0 on the dry matrix, the counts table shows no turn over 60 words that holds a point and a card, and Learning's list is in.

**Reverse if:**
- M: a model-written join contradicts the board, gives an answer away or says something untrue on more than 1 turn in the run, or more than 1 join in 5 falls back: the joins go back to the table's authored forms, rotated, and are not tried again with this model. If M and L are in and register is still below 3.5 in two topics or more: the verdict and its why are written by the model too, from the key and the authored why as facts, with the same checks.
- L: a steady lesson no longer reaches the end of its script within 170 rows, or two judges find the added check-ins tedious: the break moves to 80 words.
- H3: on more than 2 help turns in a topic a judge finds the worked answer was given where a hint would have let the child do it: the worked answer moves to the third request.
- T3: the code-picked sentence is untrue of the lesson's rows in any lesson: it is dropped and the wrap ends on the goodbye.
- P: the predicate reads 0 and a judge finds a part dropped or said twice: the predicate reads the wrong thing and is rewritten from that row before another run.
