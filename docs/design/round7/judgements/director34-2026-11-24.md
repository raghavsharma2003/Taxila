## Decision (`dc-r8-director34`, supersedes `dc-r8-director33`)

1. **Release 59 at once: a reply to the wrap's question is never graded.** It is read as the wrap's reply, as before this round. The fault was found by the automatic check on the paid run within minutes of its end; the offline check could not see it because it calls no model, and now can. *Reverses if* the closing question's own grading (a denial never right, its named wrong answer read) is lost with it; then the two readers are separated by turn, not by item.
2. **This run counts as the first of two (clause 43), under ruling 1, and nothing leaves hold on it.** The staged batches stay stopped by clauses 42, 43 and 50. `c4-maths-ch06-t01` needs the next run. *Reverses if* the next run's judges count the rows of ruling 1 as contradictions with the result boards of item 3 still missing; then this run did not count and the two start again.
3. **Every worked step that states a value is said over a board that shows the value.** The nine rows of ruling 1 and the four of ruling 3 get result boards or answer boards in their scripts; the checker's rule 2 reads the confirmation, the told line, the worked close and the child's own step, and is an error where a number, fraction or mark she states is not drawn on the board under it. The judges' prompt uses the gate's definition and nothing wider. *Reverses if* a result board gives away the step the child does next; then the sentence is cut to what the posing board shows.
4. **A second picture request gets a second picture, or the answer's reason; never a bare line.** Where the card's unsaid hint owns a board, that board is drawn as the picture and the hint is its caption; the no-other-drawing line is not said. A picture shown the turn before stays up. Only where nothing is left is the line said (the first form alone: "Iski aur koi tasveer mere paas nahi hai."), with the first unsaid of the card's hint in words, the point's other wording, or the card's own reason for a child who gave its named answer; never the key (amended 2026-11-24 before the build: as first written this said "the hint spent, the key", which breaks director32's rule that a picture request never costs the card). *Reverses if* two judges count the hint's board drawn as a picture as a contradiction or as settling its card; then those cards get an authored second picture and the hint stays words.
5. **A card may name more than one wrong answer, each with its own reason** (clause 46). The scripts name the answers the judges heard missed, starting with c4-ch01-t02 s1 e1 (a miscount), c5-t03 s2 (twelfths), c5-t01 s1's follow-up card (1/6), c4-ch06-t01's tape card and its room card, c6-ch07-t03 s1 (joining). The checker's rule 4 reads each reason against its own answer. An answer no reason names gets the hint, as now. *Reverses if* a card comes to carry more than three named answers or a reason gives its card's answer away; then that card is rewritten as two cards.
6. **Three paths in the walk.** A give-up or a "samajh nahi aaya" at a check-in between skills gets the closed card's reason in its other wording and the check-in again, never the go-on line alone (carried clause 3). A try-first turn and a bare "haan" count as turns at the question: she waits ("Haan, pehle tum try karo.") or says the short ask, never the whole card. A question is heard at most four times in a lesson in all its forms before its answer is given. A right answer whose close cannot share a turn with the next card ends on the go-on line, not a check-in. *Reverses if* the four-times limit gives answers to children who were about to get there (two judges); then the limit counts only turns after help.
7. **Fixed forms.** "Suno," and "Dekho," are not said between a right verdict and its reason. A reply to a child's side-topic is written per kind of thing said (a person, a thing done, a liking, a time), so that a child who names who was with her is not told she likes it; "Yeh baat toh do baar aa gayi." goes. The goodbye's reply is chosen from what the child said, checked on a run with the model in it. A script's first teaching turn is 60 words or fewer; an explanation frame is heard once in a skill. *Reverses if* register still does not move in two runs; then the next round rebuilds how a turn is put together from its parts, since length and shape are its largest cause in seven of nine reports.
8. **Script rows by topic** (Learning 023): the rows of mechanism 7.
9. **The cold read gains a fifth pass, and reads joints.** Each remediation and each reason is read against the wrong answer that routes a child to it; each joint form is read against the turn it can follow.
10. **The record:** the three labels of mechanism 8.
11. **The prompt is checked by code before a run.** Every sentence the prompt quotes from a script is searched for in the scripts as run, and every claim of the form "no … holds" is checked by a search; the list of allowed endings is generated from the walk, not typed.
12. **director35:** the seven live lessons and the two staged; the second of the two runs.

**What would reverse the whole:** as director33 had it. A third run in which a lesson that passed fails on something the round's own build introduced: then a round changes code only, the next scripts only.
