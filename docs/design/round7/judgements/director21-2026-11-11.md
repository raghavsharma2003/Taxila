## Decision: dc-r8-director21 (the order of a lesson is a law; the ending and the seams are authored)

Twenty-one runs have each fixed the last run's faults and met new ones, because the walk decides what comes next in code paths that are fixed one case at a time. This run's five contradictions and three unsaid boards are all one kind: something was said out of its order, or in a place that has no order. The decision is to make the order a small set of laws that hold on every turn, checked on every live row, and to move the walk onto the compiled cells so that what she says in each place is authored and not assembled.

**O, the order laws (Brain; each a predicate that fails on director21's rows first, then gated dry and run over every live run).**
- O1. A unit's parts are said in order and none is skipped: a card is never said before its point, a step never before its problem, a then-card never before its remediation. Whatever a turn displaces (a return, an AI line, a request) is said whole on the next turn.
- O2. A return, an AI line and a request reply never take a part's place. They are said, and the pending part follows in the same turn or the next.
- O3. A remediation is said only after the wrong answer that shows its misconception, on its own then-card's board. It is never used as help.
- O4. A picture request, a Hindi request, a repeat and a clarify stay on the part they were asked about. None moves the lesson on and none changes the unit.
- O5. A turn with no unit says nothing about a figure, and no board of another unit is up while it does: the teach-back, the wrap and the goodbye clear the board or show their own.
- O6. The first miss on a card never gets the key. A clarify does not spend a card's help.
- O7. Her answer to the child's question comes first in its turn (the AI line is not held behind four sentences of teaching).

**E1, the ending is authored (Learning writes, Brain says by code).** Each script gets a `teachback` (one question from the lesson with its key, its accept list and its own board, or no board) and a `wrap` (two sentences that say what the lesson covered, never what the child did, then the goodbye). The model writes none of it. A non-answer at the teach-back gets the key and its why, once.

**C1, second wordings (Learning writes, Brain selects).** Every point, every why and every worked problem gets a second wording that shows it another way (a different picture or a smaller number, not the first wording shortened). The cells become: first clarify, the simpler; second clarify, the second wording with its board; third, she says the answer plainly and moves on, and the part is marked for return. After a key, a give-up or clarify gets the why's second wording once; the same sentence is never said in two turns running (predicate: no authored sentence of 6 or more words in two consecutive turns of hers, except a card the child asked to hear again).

**J1, the seams (clause 2, applied in two steps).** The clause allowed the reply model to join authored parts. Two of three judges put the cause in joints that exist and did not fire, or do not exist; so the first step is the authored one, consistent with `dc-r8-selection-before-generation`:
- `backTo` fires on every return to an earlier card (it fired 0 times in 33; a bug);
- four new joints, three forms each, written by Learning: after her aside reply and before the lesson; after a miss verdict and before a remediation; after "try it first" and before the card; after the AI line and before the lesson;
- a predicate that no two unit parts or a reply and a part meet in a turn without a joint between them.
The second step is the clause as written: if register is still below 3.5 in two or more topics in the next run, the reply model writes the join, under `authoredWhole`.

**S2, re-asks (clause 3).** Learning marks `ask` on every card whose last sentence cannot be answered from the board and that sentence alone (a pronoun pointing back, a bare "Kya woh sahi hai?", givens not drawn). The re-ask is the `ask`. After the second miss the whole card is said.

**R2 and R3, the aside reply.** It is said once, on the turn after the child's answer, and never again (predicate). Learning rewrites the reply forms so the slot takes the child's own phrase of up to three words and the form says it back as something about the child ("Achha, tumhe bowling karna pasand hai."); a form may restate what the child said about themselves and still states no fact of the world, no taste of hers and no promise. "sixer", "chauka", "catch" and the like join the sport match list.

**D, the reader.** The child's reply to her return question and to her AI-line question has its own column and is never read as an answer, a repeat or a misconception. A give-up after a key is a give-up.

**Data (Learning), before the next run:** a friend's verb agrees with the friend's name (a predicate over every `{friend}` slot); bridges are true whatever path the lesson took (they say what comes next, not what was done); a hook's things are used again or the hook is cut; the twins check reads every pair of words for one thing in a script, not only the listed twins; Studio's picture lines use the script's own terms.

**The compile.** Step 3 goes ahead for every column, not only clarify: the walk reads the compiled cell for what to say, and the order laws decide what comes next. The old per-case code for a column is removed in the commit that switches it.

**What stays as it is.** Every park kept and returned to (P1). The authored reply library (R1). The four listening cells (L1 to L4), with L4 changed so a Hindi request on words she has just said gets the second wording. The question-only re-ask after a hint. The steady lesson to the end of the script. A remediation's say played over its then-card's board (one unit; c6's judge asked: it is allowed).

**Scale gate after director21:** not met. c5-t01 meets it. The staged 30 stay held from children. Production (release 26) carries this runtime, faults included; nobody is using it.

**Reverse if:**
- O (order laws): two judges in the next run find a child kept waiting, or a lesson that feels rigid, because a displaced part was said after its moment had passed (count: more than 2 such rows in a topic): then a displaced point may be dropped when the child has already answered its card right.
- E1: the authored wrap reads as a form letter to two or more judges: then the wrap keeps its authored first sentence and the model writes one sentence about what the child did, checked against the lesson's rows.
- C1: second wordings are no better than repeats in the next run (more than half of second clarifies judged unhelpful): then a second clarify goes straight to the worked answer with the board.
- J1: as written above (the model joins if register stays below 3.5 in two or more topics).
- R3: a restated phrase misstates the child in more than 1 reply in 10: then the slot goes back to one word.
