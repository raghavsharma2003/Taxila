# The causes under test in round d38's pair run (order `docs/patches/to-brain/055-director37-the-cap.md`, section "As built")

The scripts are word for word director37's. The walk does two things differently.

## Cause 1: the cap (when she stops asking a question and tells the answer)

Director37's judges found the cap wrong both ways: the answer told at the fourth hearing while an easier wording of the
question was still unsaid (c6-maths-ch06-t01 steering 22 and three other lessons), and one step question heard eight times
before it was told (c4-maths-ch01-t02 offtopic 22 to 46). Now:
- **One more wording before telling.** Where she would tell the answer at a question heard four times and a help wording
  of it is still unsaid, her turn is that wording, once, ending on an invitation ("Ab tum batao." / "Chalo, ab tumhari
  baari."), not on the question. At the child's next miss, give-up or non-answer the answer is told with its reason.
- **No fifth hearing on her own.** At four hearings she does not say the question again in any form (the question, its
  re-ask, its Hindi form, help ending on it) unless the child asks for the question itself ("phir se bolo"). A child's request
  for help there is helped and the help ends on an invitation.
- **Two places a fifth hearing is allowed, once each:** coming back from a side-topic (her reply, a back-to-work line, one
  new help wording, the question), and coming back from a remediation's follow-up card to the card it was for.
- **A child who only says "haan"** at a question heard four times: if she has helped at that question before, the answer is
  told; if she never has, one help first.
- A back-to-work line is not said in two of her turns running.

## Cause 2: the step read after help (a fault of the walk, fixed)

In a worked problem done together she asks the child each step ("Tumhari baari: …"). Until this round, once she had HELPED
at such a step, the child's next line was not read as an answer to the step at all: a right answer was asked again. The
rule-based children of every earlier run answer only the question the walk holds open, so after help at a step they said
their next scripted line instead (a side-topic, a request): no run before this one shows a child answering a step after
help. Now the step stays open after help: a right answer gets the step's confirm, a wrong one is a miss. Because of this
the offtopic and stuck lessons of both topics take a different path from director37 from the first help at a step onward.
A lesson taking another path is not itself a fault or a merit: judge what she says on it.
