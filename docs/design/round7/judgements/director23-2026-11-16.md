## The safety turn fired on a fraction answer (found, not changed)

In c5-t01 the card asks which mark is the place of 1/9 after saying "Kuch log sochte hain ki line par 1/9 ki koi jagah nahi hoti". The test child's wrong answer is the script's own listed value, "no place". In both lessons where it was said (steady 49, stuck 83) she gave the safety turn: helpline numbers, "Kya tum abhi surakshit ho?", and in steady a second turn naming "galat sparsh". In stuck the lesson ended there. In director22 the same line on the same card got a hint.

The cause is in `server/director/classify.js`: a line that code has already matched against the card's answer table is still sent, alone and with no lesson context, to the model's words-only distress read (`needsModelDistressRead`, `distressCheck`), whose prompt lists being frightened of "a place". The model said yes to "no place" twice in this run and no in the last one. No code changed here between the runs; the read is a model's and it varies.

This is the safety code the owner froze on 2026-10-27 (keep it as it is: do not remove, extend or measure it). I have not changed it and have not measured how often it happens. It is recorded as a finding and put to the owner with a recommendation: skip the words-only read when the line is exactly an answer the card's own table lists (the rule that exact answers under three words already have). Until the owner answers, the test child keeps saying "no place", so every run shows whether it fired.

## Decision: dc-r8-director23 (one seam pass that the predicates read; the reader's record is what the walk acted on)

Supersedes `dc-r8-director22`. Its rules P, T, B, K, H, W and A stand as written, with the additions below.

**S, the seam pass (Brain; first).** No code path adds a joint. When a turn's parts are assembled, one function walks each pair of neighbouring parts and decides the seam from a table keyed by the two kinds and what came between: reply into lesson, miss verdict into remediation, verdict into a return to an earlier card (in the same turn or the next), Hindi switch, park, bridge. It puts at most one joint at a seam, and none between a point and its own card or between a verdict and its own why. A filler word that opens two neighbouring parts ("Achha") is said once. Forms rotate by a seed from the lesson id and the count of that joint so far, so two lessons do not open with the same form and one lesson does not say a form twice in four of her turns; the same rotation covers the why's opener after a verdict ("Dekho,") and the verdict openers. The function writes the turn's seams on the row (`seams`: for each pair, the two kinds, the joint kind or none, the form). The seam predicates read that list and nothing else; `backToJoint` and `missRemediationJoint` are rewritten on it, and a new one counts a required seam with no joint and a joint at a seam that takes none.

**R2, the reader's record (Brain).** The row's `column` is what the walk acted on, written after every re-read, never the classifier's first label. Her reply after a return or an AI-line question is a reply: she answers it and goes on from where the lesson stood; nothing already said is said again because of it. A give-up or a clarify after a key's check-in is recorded as that. A line after the closing key's check-in is never graded. `readerAgrees` loses its equivalence table: the intent tag and the column match by name, and the only pairs allowed are listed in the predicate with a reason each. Brain also checks what the learner record received on the 11 give-up rows; if any wrote a misconception as evidence, that is a misgrade and is fixed first.

**C, captions (Learning authors, Brain wires).** Each board that a picture request can redraw gets an authored caption for its view: one or two sentences that say what to look at in the picture and name nothing a later card asks for. c6's captions are the model. The picture turn says the caption and then the card's question alone, never the whole card. The line that names nothing stays as the fallback and the counts table shows how often it is said; the target is 0 in the four pilots.

**T2, the ending (Brain).** The sentence about the child is written to the child, in the second person; a sentence that names her in the third person is not said. It is about the hardest thing she got right, with the closing question first if she got it right. A miss on the closing question gets a miss verdict before the key. When the child says something after the key's check-in, the wrap opens with one short authored acknowledgement.

**H2, help never repeats (Brain).** No help of any kind (clarify, give-up, Hindi, hint) says a sentence she said in her last two turns. At a point's check-in the help is the point's second wording or simpler, never a card's and never the next card. A give-up on a card gets that card's own hint. A second clarify with nothing unsaid left goes to the worked answer with the board.

**Boards (Brain, Learning).** A remediation that has its own authored board has it drawn before she describes it (c4 steady 98). Learning draws the gate on c6's s3 e3 board.

**K2 (Brain).** After a wrong answer to a yes-or-no card the key is one sentence that states the fact ("Dono barabar nahi hain, kyunki…"), never a miss verdict followed by "Haan, kyunki".

**A2 (Brain).** Her AI line always ends with her question back, and a second or third aside in a lesson gets a reply in the child's words, not "Theek hai."

**Learning.** The giveaways, the second whys that contain the key, the unnatural sentences and the word pairs listed in the four reports; the return line "Toh ungli kul kitne cm chali?"; the park form that uses "chakkar".

**Harness (Brain).** `cardWholeAgain` and `hindiNewWords` are checked against the rows the judges list and fixed to count them. The false-key flag on c5-t02 steady 132 and the five `contradictionsV6` flags on c4's own answer boards are removed or corrected.

**What stays as it is.** The order laws. Pictures always draw. The answer board at the first word of the key. Bridges. Try-first. Re-asks (every hint in four topics was answerable from the board and her last turn). Grading against verified keys (0 misgrades in 294 verdicts). The safety code.

**Scale gate after director23:** not met by any topic. The staged 30 stay held from children.

**director24 runs when** the seam predicates read 0 on the dry matrix from the `seams` list, `readerAgrees` reads 0 with no equivalence table, the counts table shows 0 quiet picture lines in the four pilots, and Learning's list is in.

**Reverse if:**
- S: with the seam pass in, a judge in director24 counts more than 2 joint faults in a topic, or register is below 3.5 in two topics or more: then the reply model writes the joins from the `seams` list, with the authored parts checked intact by code. No further deferral, and no departure from the number this time.
- R2: `readerAgrees` reads 0 with no equivalences and a judge counts more than 3 wrong records in a topic: then the intent tags are wrong and the test children are rewritten before another run.
- C: more than 1 authored caption in the run gives away a later answer or says something the picture does not show: then captions are generated from the board's parameters by code and Learning's are dropped.
- T2: the sentence about the child is absent, in the third person, wrong or empty in more than 1 lesson in 4 that reach the wrap: then it is replaced by a fact picked by code and said in an authored form.
- Picture turns by the question alone: on more than 2 picture turns in a topic a judge finds the child could not answer without the card's givens: then a picture turn says the card whole again.
- H2: more than 1 help turn in a topic leaves the child with nothing new: then a second request for help of any kind goes to the worked answer.
