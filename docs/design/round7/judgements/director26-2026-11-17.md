## What fails now, by mechanism

1. **A remediation's help is drawn over another card's board.** The help function returns the wording; for a remediation with no board of its own, or said after its board, the page is built from the skill's first check card with the same mix-up. Six rows, the whole of the run's count failures.
2. **Check-ins in the worked examples.** A told step is 35 to 40 words with a "kyunki" clause, so two never fit in a turn, and each ends on a check-in the steady child answers "haan" to. Also: a check-in after a verdict where the next part fitted (c4 steady 74, 86, 92), and a check-in on every help wording at a step.
3. **The go-on line is said where no key was given, and stacked.** At a worked step whose ladder is empty she says "aisa sawaal aage phir aayega; ab aage chalte hain" and then works the same problem (c5-t02 stuck 58, 64; c5-t01 stuck 44; c6 stuck 44). Before a return to an earlier card it is followed at once by the back-to joint, two joints that contradict each other (c5-t01 stuck 30, steering 18; c5-t02 stuck 46; c6 stuck 30, steering 18).
4. **The stop path says the model's draft** (above).
5. **A request with no part recorded still gets nothing or the model.** A give-up after the check-in on the last card's why went straight to the closing question (c5-t01 steady 128). A clarify at the opening's check-in got a turn the model drafted from the first point, cut short and ending on no question (c5-t02 steering 4; the run's own `endsOnQuestion`, `orderParts` and `orderDisplaced` flags). A give-up at the hook gets the first point with no word to the child (c6, all four lessons).
6. **A mix-up met again in a later skill gets no remediation.** The steady child made the same mistake four times in c5-t02's third skill and that skill's own remediation was never said, because the mix-up had been remediated in the first skill.
7. **No lead-in to a worked problem.** A verdict runs straight into the problem (c6 six turns; c5-t02 four), and the problem is worded as a question the child then answers "haan" to.
8. **Replies went flat.** "Oh, chauka. Achha." and a bare "Achha." to an aside said again (c4 offtopic 20, 32; c6 stuck 54; c5-t02 steady 40), worse than director25 in three topics.
9. **Script.** The six hints above. Second wordings that paraphrase (16 help turns). The teachback has no hint, so a near miss gets the key at once (c6 steady 112). The closing verdict's why is not said (c4 steady 128; c5-t01 steady 130). "hissa" for a fraction part and for a bit of the lesson; "upar" in two senses. A raw key slot ("Is sawaal ka sahi jawab: pinky ka").
10. **The record.** Help wordings recorded as "point"; a verdict recorded on go-on turns where none was said; `partOffBeat` raised on 41 rows of which 3 are real; `orderParts` false on c5-t02 steering 6.

## Decision: dc-r8-director26 (help's board is its part's on every path; worked steps two to a turn; one joint at a seam; no request gets nothing)

Supersedes `dc-r8-director25`. H4's picker and ladder, the return turn, the two-turn wrap, the break at 75 words with the point-and-card split at 60, and everything under "what stays" stand.

**B1, a help wording's board (clauses 3, 12).** The help function returns the board with the wording, and nothing downstream may replace it. A remediation's help is said over the remediation's own board. A remediation with no board of its own keeps the board that is up; it never borrows a card's. Learning gives every remediation in the four pilots a board. The page builder takes the board from the help decision and from nowhere else, and throws in a test run if a help page's board is not the one the function returned.

**B2, the stop path (clause 9).** A child who wants to stop gets the authored line and nothing the model wrote: no buttons named, the teacher's own gender, the three choices in her words. The rewrite path that keeps a draft a guard flagged is closed here as it was for lessons.

**C1, check-ins (clause 4).** A worked example's told steps are said two to a turn where they fit in 75 words, with one check-in at the end; Learning shortens every told step to 30 words or fewer with at most one "kyunki". A check-in is not added after a verdict and its why when the next part fits (built in director25's round; not followed on c4 steady 74, 86, 92). A help wording at a step is followed by the step's own check-in, not a second one. Measured on the dry matrix before release, by the measure in clause 4's row.

**J1, one joint at a seam, and the go-on line in its place.** The go-on line is said only after a key. At a worked step whose ladder is empty she goes to the next step with the step joint the script already has, and no promise. The go-on line is a joint of the seam table: where the back-to joint follows, the go-on line is not said. A new joint before a worked problem (`beforeWorked`, three authored forms), and a worked problem is worded as a problem to do together, not as a question to the child.

**N1, no request gets nothing.** When no part is recorded, the part is the last part she said, and help is picked for it. A give-up or clarify at the opening's check-in is answered by the first point through the authored path, ending on its check-in, with one authored word to the child first; the model drafts no teaching turn. A give-up after the last card's why gets the why's other wording or the go-on line before the closing question.

**R1, a skill's remediation is its own.** "Said once" is counted for each remediation, not for each mix-up: a mix-up met again in a later skill gets that skill's remediation.

**A4, replies.** A second or third aside, and an aside said again, get a reply with the child's word in one of the authored forms; "Oh, X. Achha." and a bare "Achha." are not replies.

**W1, the wrap.** The closing verdict says its why. Learning shortens each `covered` sentence to 20 words or fewer and writes acknowledgements that fit any answer to the closing question, a give-up included.

**Predicates.** `helpBoard` is rewritten from the six director26 rows and a test requires it to read non-zero on each; the director25 rows stay in the test. `partOffBeat` is made to mean what it says or is removed from the row. New gated predicates, each with a test on the director26 rows named above: the go-on line only after a key; no two joints at one seam; a stop turn is the authored line; no teaching turn without parts; a worked step's turn holds two steps where they fit. The record: a help wording is recorded as help, and no verdict is recorded where none was said.

**Learning.** A board for every remediation. An independent read and rewrite of every hint in the four pilots against its card: a hint points at a method, never states the numbers of the answer, and is true of the board (clause 7's six named first). The first skill's `pointAlt`s and every `problemAlt` rewritten so they are not the first wording again. Told steps of 30 words or fewer. `covered` sentences of 20 words or fewer. Wrap acknowledgements. Three `beforeWorked` forms. A hint for each teachback. "hissa" and "upar" in one sense per turn. The raw key slot in c4.

**What stays as it is.** The order laws. The seam table and its record. The reader's record. Pictures always draw, with authored captions and the question alone. The answer board at the first word of the key. Bridges, try-first, re-asks. The sentence about the child. Grading against verified keys. The safety code. The model's joins off.

**Not decided here, recorded as the next teaching question:** the worked examples are told to the child, who does one step at the end; three runs of judges have said so under "what a parent would still find wrong". Making each told step end on a small question of its own, with a key, would turn the check-ins into work. It changes the script format and the walk, so it waits until four topics pass the gate as it stands.

**Scale gate after director26:** met by c5-t01. Not met by c4 and c6 (the help board alone) or c5-t02 (the help board and register). The staged 30 stay held from children.

**Who builds it.** As in the last round: agents run from the main loop (messages to the stream sessions still wait on the owner). One agent for the code, because the last round's two parallel builds met badly at the merge; one for the scripts.

**director27 runs when** B1, B2, C1, J1, N1 and R1 are in with their predicates at 0 on the dry matrix and non-zero on the director26 rows, C1's measure is met on the dry matrix, and Learning's list is in.

**Reverse if:**
- B1: any help wording over a board that is not its part's in director27. Then the page for a help turn is no longer built from the walk's move at all: help turns carry their own page, built by the help function.
- C1: the dry measure is not met, or after director27 two judges still find the check-ins tedious: the break of turns without a card is dropped, as clause 4 first said, and the worked example's steps are told in one turn.
- J1: a judge finds a seam with no joint where the go-on line used to stand and the turn reads abrupt on more than 2 rows in a topic: a step joint form is authored for that place.
- N1: a request still gets nothing on any row: the walk refuses to end a turn without a help decision when the child's line was a request, and the row that did it is the test.
- R1: a judge finds the same remediation said twice in a lesson, or a remediation said for a mix-up the child did not show: the count goes back to the mix-up, and the later skill's cards get a hint for it instead.
- Register below 3.5 in two topics or more: the cause with the most rows in director27's reports gets the round.
