## What v5 fixed (consensus)

- **Bridges are spoken** at every change of skill. All bridges reached were spoken: 2 of 2 in c4 and c5-t01, 3 of 3 in c5-t02 and c6. director7 spoke 0 of 5.
- **No "Yaad karo" recap over a different board.** director7 had 3–7 per topic.
- **The then-card's board no longer lags a turn.** Closes come before the switch.
- **Requests are no longer graded.** "sawaal kya hai", "phir se bolo" and "kis cheez ka jawab dun" re-pose the card in 9 of 9 c4 cases and 4 of 4 c5-t02 cases. Try-first hands the card over.
- **False keys from `keyInLang` are gone.** "jawab 2 tha" became "2 aur 3 ke beech tha".
- **Data fixes landed:**
  - c4 s1-e3's ask fits its board;
  - the c5-t02 remediation pairs no longer repeat the check pairs;
  - the c6 skill-1 then-cards use fresh figures.
- **No "Virat wali baat mujhe yaad hai" stubs.** The language mode held in c5-t02 (4/4).

## Decision: dc-r7-script-walk-v6 (every sentence on its own board; requests and parks done for real)

The teaching and grading layer passes for the third run in a row, and none of the four judges asks for a format change. **The reversal is not relevant.** The runtime contradiction class fell by more than half (51 → about 22). The largest single cause is now a Studio regression.

**Runtime** (Brain):
- **R1. Every sentence on its own board.** A sentence about the previous card (verdict, reason, close) is a segment on that card's board, including a right-answer verdict with a reason. A verdict is never inside the closure segment. The worked close is said once. A bridge is its own segment on its authored board; until that board exists, the bridge does not point at the screen.
- **R2. A close's "why" comes only from the closed card's own authored why.** Never the last sentence of a point or remediation.
- **R3. False keys.** The `invented` guard replaces the line. Any number she states as an answer is checked against the key and accept forms, not only the `jawab X tha` phrasing.
- **R4. Requests are moves.**
  - "samajh nahi aaya" / "pata nahi": a *smaller* re-explanation, never a verbatim repeat or the next step.
  - "phir se bolo" is never a miss.
  - A picture request redraws the live card's board, and she describes only what is drawn.
  - A repeated miss gets a hint naming the specific missing piece.
  - A probe answer gets one acknowledging line.
- **R5. Real returns.** A parked aside gets one line of real content at the promised point, never a stub, a false analogy or a misattribution. A language mode visibly holds for the rest of the lesson.
- **R6. Render hygiene.**
  - One verdict sentence per turn.
  - No duplicate sentence, and no question asked twice in one turn.
  - A worked lead-in never states the result before it is derived.
  - About 60 words per turn at most, enforced by splitting into turns, not by truncating.
  - No echo of the child's English.
  - Rephrase "jawab nahi tha".

**Studio:**
- **S1′.** Only the value a card asks for may be "?". A number the spoken card or prompt states is always drawn, even when it also appears in an accept form. Once confirmed, an answer is revealed on the board.
- **S3′.** A picture request draws the live card's own figure. It never draws nothing and never redraws an unrelated board.
- A test over the four pilot scripts plus the 30 staged ones: for every check and then-card, no number the prompt states is drawn as "?".

**Data, v2.2 fixes; still no format change** (Learning):
- an authored `why` for every ask, check and then-card key;
- a board for every `bridgeToNext`, e.g. a compare board "8 ka 1/2 = 4 | 8 ka 1/4 = 2";
- the asked figure drawn (c5-t01's 7-mark line);
- worked and then-card boards shade the parts the line says were eaten or taken;
- c5-t01 s1-e3 and s3-e2 carry their must-says;
- c5-t02's then-card boards show the sizes compared;
- c6: a 9×2 distractor with one meaning, a fresh figure for the s3 remediation then-card, and s3-e3's gate drawn.

**Harness** (Brain, with Learning):
- the per-segment checker models "?" against stated numbers, picture lines against what is drawn, a "why" from another board, and a verdict in the wrong segment;
- each segment's actual board id and time are logged;
- probe replies are never counted as keyed answers;
- child events fire only in context;
- offtopic and stuck lessons run long enough to reach skill 2.

**Not built:** Brain's code-classified speculation measured slower overall (p50 1,954 vs 1,852 ms; it displaced the 970 ms early-clause path), so `TAXILA_CODE_SPEC` stays off. The streamed, held-clause version is queued until after v6.
