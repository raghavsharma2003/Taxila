## Decision: dc-r8-director28 (a point and its card share a turn again, with the short reason making room; quick questions and the invitation go; every point's and remediation's board is its own; help is a hard precondition for a request)

The round's two new devices failed for the same reason: each put a new kind of question between the child and the card, and neither asked anything. The remedy is fewer kinds of turn, not better small questions.

**The turn shapes after this decision.** A teaching turn ends on one of: a card's question, an asked worked step, the child's own step, or a check-in after help, after a key or after a bridge. Nothing else.

1. **A point and its card share a turn** (clause 4). After a right answer the turn is verdict, reason, point, card; the short reason is said when the full one leaves no room. The walk's 60-word cap on a point after a verdict goes. The scripts are cut so every point and its card fit (Learning 017 sets the limits; the checker enforces them). A quick question is asked only where a script keeps one, and a script keeps one only where a point and its card cannot be cut to fit and the question's answer is not said in the point (the checker reads that).
2. **The hook, the first point and its card are one turn.** The hook never ends on a check-in, so no request can meet it, and the switch line is never said before a first telling.
3. **A remediation and its follow-up card are one turn where both fit 60 words** (clause 4), and the remediations are cut to fit. The remediation is said over its own board; the card's board comes at the card's first word.
4. **The verdict's turn runs into the worked example** (clause 4): verdict, short reason, lead-in, problem, and the first question. No invitation. The scripts put the first question straight after the problem.
5. **Worked examples ask the reasoning step.** The asked step is the one where the child decides what to do; the sum can be told. The child's own step needs thinking. No fact is said twice.
6. **Every point's and every remediation's board is its own** (clause 1): it shows what that part says, and nothing its card asks that the part does not speak of. The count-marks card's board loses its ticks.
7. **A joint keeps what is up** (clause 1). The page of a part starts at that part's first word on every path: the try-first joint, the closing lead-in, the switch line, a bridge, a hook.
8. **Help is a hard precondition** (clause 3, clause 11). A turn that answers a request says an unsaid wording of the last part taught, or its key, or the "nothing easier" line, and only then anything else. After a key the "nothing easier" line comes before the return. After an aside reply or a park, "the last part taught" is the one before it. The walk throws under tests when a request's turn has none of the three.
9. **An aside reply or a park never ends on a check-in.** It goes on into the live part with its joint. The reply and its joint (15 words at most) are not counted in the turn's 75.
10. **"Let me try" at an asked step means that step:** she says the step's question again. No step is skipped. A wrong answer to an asked step hears a short authored "not that" before the step is told.
11. **The record** says what was heard (mechanism 12).

**What is not changed:** the picker and its ladder; the key over the answer board; the closing question's path; the return turn and its back-to line; the two-turn wrap; the break at 75 words; the reader; the short reason as built; `holdAuthored`.

**Work orders:** `docs/patches/to-learning/017-director28-boards-and-fit.md` (items 1 to 6 in the scripts, the checker's limits) and `docs/patches/to-brain/044-fewer-kinds-of-turn.md` (items 1 to 4 and 7 to 11 in the walk).

**The staged 30 stay held.** They now also need: every point's and remediation's board read against its part, the fit limits, worked examples with a reasoning step asked, and no echo question.

## What would reverse this

- Two director29 judges finding the turns of verdict, reason, point and card too much in one breath → the limit for that turn goes to 65 and the scripts are cut again; quick questions do not come back.
- Two judges finding the remediation with its card too much in one breath again (director27's finding) → the remediation ends on the card's question only when both fit 50; otherwise it stands alone and ends on a check-in.
- Any topic with an unsaid board from a joint → the page's start is taken from the part's first word in one place for every path, and the predicate is written from that function's output.
- Any request answered without one of the three → the path is named and the precondition is moved to where the turn is assembled.
- Two judges finding right answers under-explained where the short reason is said → the short form is used only when the next part is a point of its own skill.
- A worked example that two judges read as a quiz of sums again → that script's examples are rewritten with the judge's rows before anything is scaled.
- Two topics passing the gate with every axis at 3.5 → the staged scripts get the same read, three at a time, each judged before the next three.
