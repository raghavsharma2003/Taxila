## Decision: dc-r7-script-walk-v6-9 (the held board stays; the turns that teach nothing hold it too)

**Clauses applied, as set before the run:**
1. **Held cost:** 0. Not triggered. `dc-r7-held-board` stays as it is.
2. **Listening below 3.5 with A4 and B1‴ in place:** triggered in all four topics. **Request handling moves to an explicit table.**
3. **Any misgrade:** none. Not triggered.
4. **H5 misses more than 1:** it missed 13 of 16. **The counter is retired as a gate input.** Hand judging is the only screen. Brain keeps it only as a development aid, and no README or message quotes it as evidence.

**Brain, v6.9:**
- **N1, a turn with no unit part draws nothing new.** The intent on aside questions, aside returns, acknowledgements, break menus, stop menus and goodbyes is the board that is up, with no `switchTo`, and the walk does not advance to the next unit on such a turn. The four held predicates are made to throw on these paths, with director17's nine rows as fixtures.
- **N2, the bridge belongs to the next card's unit.** A bridge after a give-up switches at its first spoken part, like any other.
- **T1, the request table.** One table, request × beat kind → one response shape, in code, reviewed row by row, with a test per row built from the director17 rows above. It covers at least:
  - Hindi and clarify on a point, a worked step, a remediation, an open card, and a card whose simpler is spent: the live part re-said in different authored words, never the same sentence, never a skip ahead; with nothing left to re-say, the key and its why.
  - A miss after a spent simpler: the key.
  - "pata nahi" at a check-in: the card is posed first; its simpler comes only after a miss on it.
  - A request (picture, repeat, try-first, Hindi) arriving where an aside reply is expected: served as that request.
  - An aside when no card is open: answered or parked without "is sawaal ke baad"; a repeated aside is returned to once, never "likh li hai" and silence.
  - "haan" or a bare acknowledgement during a worked step: the next step.
- **A5, the acknowledgement after an aside reply** is said only when the child's reply is about the aside, and has three or more forms. Otherwise the reply is handled by the table.
- **R6, wording:** authored lines are never re-inflected; one step opener per turn; no step opener before the problem; the picture line ends with a full stop, keeps the card's "?", and writes fractions as words.

**Studio:**
- **S10, the split second view** is built only from the held board's own parts: no bar, mark or count the board does not have. c5-t01's e2 is the fixture.
- **S11, the picture line** says "barabar" for equal parts and "alag-alag" only for unequal ones, and never erases a contrast the card's prompt names.
- **The seam draws only what the intent holds** when there is no `switchTo` (the other half of N1).
- **The harness records the split layout** on each picture row (patch to Brain).

**Learning, D12:**
- Bridges lose their `bridgeBoard` and are written to be true of the next card's board.
- c5-t02: the s1 worked board hides the yourStep's answer; s1 c2 is posed on unnamed bars; e1's why no longer states e2's key; s2 c1's answer board is shaded.
- c4: each s2 point models one grouping on its own grid before its ask, and e1's grid suits a halving question.
- c6: the l-plus-b remediation's counter line is made true of its board.
- The validator checks that no worked or ask board shows the answer its next card asks for.

It is nine turns that teach nothing and still moved the board, two bridges, and Studio's new split view on one card.
