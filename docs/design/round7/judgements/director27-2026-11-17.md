## What fails now, by mechanism

1. **A turn's board is not chosen part by part** (all the count failures but one). Four shapes of one fault:

   - a remediation with no board in the script borrows its follow-up card's board (c5-t02 steady 118; c4 and c6, four rows each);
   - a card posed after the AI line keeps the board that was up; its own page is dropped (c6 offtopic 10).
2. **A request can still end without help** (c5-t01 listening 3). After a key, the back-to line replaced the go-on line and nothing answered the child (stuck 30, steering 18); a fourth clarify at a worked step got the next step (stuck 44). At a bridge's check-in the address still points at the card before it, so a request there gets that card's explanation and is recorded as "after a key" (c5-t01 steady 80 and 82; c6 stuck 58, offtopic 54).
3. **The closing question gives its key at the first miss** while its hint is unsaid (c5-t02 steady 126, c6 steady 108).
4. **Check-ins where the child could do something** (all four judges). The two kinds every judge names: a verdict with a new point whose card waits (6 a steady lesson, each followed by "haan" and then the card alone in 13 to 25 words), and the worked problem said alone with a step check-in before any step. The steady child says "haan" 16 to 22 times a lesson.
5. **A remediation and its card in one turn is too much in one breath** (c5-t01 and c5-t02 judges: two new stories and a new question in 68 to 74 words). A point with its card is not (no judge).
6. **Seams left abrupt:** a return to an earlier card with no back-to line (7 rows), an aside reply straight into a worked problem (c4 steady 46), the hook into the first point (c6 steady 2, offtopic 2).
7. **Small paths:** the hook said alone after a request at the greeting (8 lessons), and in one the first point never said (c5-t02 offtopic 2); a picture asked for after a key gets a code caption (three rows); the same aside reply said twice (c4 offtopic 20 and 32); a switch line followed by words that are not easier or are another part's (c4 stuck 10, c5-t01 stuck 8 and steering 8, c5-t02 stuck 10, c6 steering 4 and 8); "thak gayi, bye" answered with the goodbye alone.

9. **The record:** the opener before the first point and the stop line are recorded as "aside", the closing question's lead-in as "point", and the closing verdict is flagged `closeOffBoard` when it is over its own answer board (c4 steady 138, c5-t01 steady 128).

## Decision: dc-r8-director27 (a turn's board is chosen part by part; a request always gets help for the part she is on; where a check-in asked "samajh aaya?" before a waiting card or a worked step, the child now answers a small keyed question)

Three work orders, in this order. The staged 30 stay held.

**Brain 041: faults, no format change.**

- **P1, one paging law.** A turn's pages are built from the parts said in it. A part with a board of its own pages that board at its own first word. A part with no board keeps what is up. A card's board is never drawn before the card's first word, on any path (after a remediation, after the AI line, after a return, on the greeting's help path). The dry run gets general predicates for this, written from the gate's own sentences and not from this run's shapes: a board on screen belongs to a part said in this turn or earlier; a part's own board is up at its first word; a turn that says a card has the card's board. Each must be non-zero on director27's rows before the fix and zero after.
- **P2, a remediation and its card are separate turns again** (the limit for adding the card goes back to 60, which they never meet). The remediation ends on its check-in, which is the natural place for one. A point and its card keep 75.
- **P3, requests.** When the child's line is a request, the turn carries a help decision for the part she is on: a wording of that part, its key, or the authored `helpSpent` line when nothing is left. A back-to line or a next step is never the whole answer. After a bridge the address is the bridge, and a request there gets the bridge's own second wording. `keyed` means a key was said as help; a right answer's verdict does not set it. After a key, one authored form says both things: this will come again, and now back to the earlier question (Learning writes it; the two joints are not stacked).
- **P4, the closing question** goes through the same picker: its hint or second wording first, the key when none is left.
- **P5, joints.** The back-to line on every return to an earlier card, whatever led there. The lead-in before a worked problem on the aside-reply path too. The go-on line only after a key said as help.
- **P6, small paths.** After a request at the greeting, the hook is said with the first point as on the ordinary path, and the first point is always said. A picture asked for after a key gets the part's authored caption. An aside reply is not said twice; the second aside's reply uses another form. After a switch line the words are the part's own easier wording (the card's when she is on a card). A child who says she is tired and says bye gets one authored sentence about that before the goodbye.
- **P7, the record.** The opener and the stop line are their own kinds; the closing question's lead-in is not a point; `closeOffBoard` does not fire over the closing question's own answer board. The harness child answers the wrap's question with a word about the lesson, not "haan".

**Learning 016: scripts.**

- **The two new fields** (below), for the four pilot topics.
- **Re-asks.** A re-ask says the question again without doing its work: it never states a number the answer needs that the card left the child to find. c4's hints and re-asks get an independent read (clause 8).
- **Hints against the posing board.** Every hint is read against the board the card is posed on, not the picture view (c6 check 2 of skill 1).
- **Remediation boards.** Each remediation either has a board its words speak, or is plainly an "imagine" telling with no board (the walk then keeps what is up). No remediation board shows what its follow-up card asks (c5-t01 count-marks: the 11 ticks). c5-t02 skill 3's third remediation: the second wording's board fits the second wording.
- **The after-key return form** (P3), the c6 hook's last sentence leading into its first point, a second reply form for a repeated aside, and the sentence for a tired child.
- **The judges' sentences:** c4 (check 3's why "bada tukda aadha cake hai", the help at steady 84 that misstates the card, "upar/neeche" in two senses, paratha against roti, skill 1's own step trivial); c5-t01 ("3 khambon se kul 4 bane", "bata 5", "Yahi baat doosre sire se" as a hint); c5-t02 (the circular why in skill 3 step 1, "likho", "ginti" in two senses, chocolate against patti); c6 ("hissa" in two senses, cm against centimetre, line against rekha, points that name the operation before the card asks for it).

**Brain 042: the child does something where she said "haan".** Built after Learning 016 lands.

- **A quick question on a point.** `explain[].quick = { prompt, key, accept, say }`: a question of 14 words or fewer that the point just said answers, with a verified key that is not the card's key; `say` is the answer in one line of 12 words or fewer. Where a verdict, its why and a new point leave no room for the card, the turn ends on the quick question, not on a check-in. A right answer gets a short yes and the card. Anything else gets `say` and the card: no hint, no remediation, no second try, and nothing recorded against the child but the fact.
- **A worked example is done with the child.** `workedExample.steps[].ask = { prompt, key, accept, right }` on at least one step of every example with two or more steps: the question whose answer is that step's result; `right` is the confirmation said after a right answer (15 words or fewer). The problem is said with what follows it up to the first question (a step's `ask`, or the child's own step), never alone, and no turn of a worked example ends on a step check-in. A right answer gets `right` and the walk goes on; anything else gets the step told as today.
- **Measure, set now:** a steady lesson has at most 14 turns that end on a comprehension check-in (director27: 29, 26, 23, 20), none of them before a waiting card or inside a worked example, and no turn is over 75 words. The dry run counts it and a predicate holds the two "never" places at zero.

**Not changed:** the 75-word break; a point with its card to 75; the check-in after help, after a remediation, after a bridge; the frozen safety code.

**What would reverse this:**

- **P1:** any contradiction or unsaid board in director28 that is a board drawn for a part not being said → pages stop being derived from the walk's move at all: every part carries its page in the script compile, and the walk only concatenates.
- **P2:** two judges finding the separate remediation turn's check-in tedious → the remediation ends on its follow-up card when both fit 60, and the remediations are cut to fit.
- **P3:** any request in director28 answered without a help part → the reader's request kinds become a hard precondition in the walk (a turn for a request cannot be built without one), with that row as its test.
- **Brain 042, quick questions:** two judges finding them a quiz (a question that tests nothing, or a card asked twice) → they are kept only in worked examples, and the place before a waiting card is closed by shorter whys on right answers (a `whyShort` of 10 words) so the point and its card share the turn. A quick question that gives away its card's answer in any lesson → that topic's quick questions are re-read before anything is scaled. Any misgrade on a quick question → its `accept` list is the fault and quick questions go two-option only.
- **Brain 042, worked examples:** worked example below 4 in two topics → the asked step moves (it was the wrong step), not back to telling.
- **The measure:** not met in the dry run → the judged run does not go ahead until the main loop has read where the check-ins come from, as before director27.
- **The gate itself:** four topics passing every line → the staged 30 get the same script read (hints against posing boards, re-asks, remediation boards, quick questions) before any goes live.
