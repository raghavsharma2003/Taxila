## Zones

- **phone** (portrait, and any width < 900): top bar 48 · stage (slot + HUD rail 64) · answer surface (tiles / pad, only while up) · steer chips 48 · dock 112 (Young 128) · pad 8. Gutters 8.
- **wide** (≥ 900 wide, landscape, ≥ 520 tall): top 56; the stage column on the left; the side column (320-400 px, 28.000000000000004% of width): her tile, the caption, the answer surface (when it fits; her tile yields to 200 first), chips, dock 128. Gutters 24.
- **landscape** (a phone turned, < 520 tall): the wide grid without her tile; she is the PiP again.

## The slot (what Studio, games and modules draw into)

- One letterboxed slot. Pieces declare a design canvas (square 1000×1000, board 1000×760, wide 1000×625, cinema 1600×900) and fit with xMidYMid meet; letterbox bands take the stage background; nothing scrolls inside the slot.
- **PiP keep-clear zone:** the top-right square of side 26% of the slot's short side (clamped 84-128 px), inset 8 px. Pieces put no label, value or target there on phones and short landscape. On wide screens there is no PiP (she is a tile in the side column), but keeping the zone clear costs nothing and lets one certified layout serve both.
- **Label zone:** top-left 18% × 12% stays clear for the stage tag.
- **The HUD rail** sits under the slot, not over it: the question (or a game's goal rail in play mode) never covers the piece.
- **With nothing on the stage** the stage holds her (a large comms window with the caption), so it is never empty and no build state is shown. The PiP appears only while a piece holds the slot.
- **With an answer surface up** (tiles, the NumberPad), the piece stays on the stage in the smaller "with answer" box (phones). On wide screens the answer surface goes to the side column and the stage keeps its full box.
