# Galti Pakdo run shots (r7 galti-pakdo, 360 × 640, deviceScaleFactor 1)

c7-maths-ch15-t03 (Mind the mistake) in the new family galti-pakdo, mode kaapi (the friend's notebook). A classmate's
working is written line by line with its "=" signs in one column; every line is made by the law (one step done to both
sides) and a faulty working carries exactly one of the topic's own step bugs. The ⚖ on a line puts the claim x = v into
THAT line on the taraazu (borrowed from taraazu/balance: beam, spring, pans): each pan shows its side's value and, under
it, the side with v put in. Tapping a working line says it is the first that breaks; in the mend goal (sudhaaro) three
lines are then offered where the balance was, and the one tapped is weighed in the question.

Made with the play dev harness and a driver that acts ONLY through real input: page.mouse clicks at the centre of the
canvas targets in `window.__play.audit.targets`, and getByTestId(`play-ctl-<id>`) for the DOM buttons. The act lists come
from the law's own solve / mal-rule acts (read, never dispatched). Every goal was solved this way for every kind of
working on two seeds (3 and 17, looks 1 and 3), and once more on seeds 41 and 77 with the kind left to the seed; the
smallest tap target measured in every run was 44 × 44 px.
