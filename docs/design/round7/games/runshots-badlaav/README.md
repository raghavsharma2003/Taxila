# Badlaav run shots (r7 badlaav, 360 × 640, deviceScaleFactor 1)

Class 7 science, chapter 5: physical and chemical changes, rusting and combustion, in the new family badlaav ("change").
Nothing runs on a clock. A change runs, a solid goes in, a side of the fire triangle changes, a piece of fuel burns or a day
passes only when the child taps; slow changes advance by the child's own Wait or Another day. Each beat is drawn inside
busy().

No act lights a fire. The fire is a picture that responds when a side of its triangle is taken away or given more; the fan,
the lid and the wood are drawn alone, never with a hand near the flame. No act puts water on a fire.

- **palat** (c7-science-ch05-t01):
  - **nishaan** (s2, s1): a card of the kit's changes. Tap Let it happen, watch the Now picture, then mark the kit sign it
    showed (Gas bubbles, Colour change, Heat or light, A new smell, A new solid) or Nothing new. A sign is taken only if that
    change shows it.
  - **ulta** (s3, s1): let it happen, try to undo it (Cool it / Warm it), then say It came back / Same stuff, not back / A new
    substance. Torn paper is on every card: it does not come back and it is still paper.
  - **ghol** (s4): put each solid on the warm tawa (no water) or into the cold water on a kitchen scale; Wait or Stir a step at
    a time; Done. The scale's pointer moves when sugar or salt goes in and stays there as it dissolves.
- **aag** (c7-science-ch05-t02):
  - **trikon** (s2, s1): a sigri beside its fire triangle (Fuel, Oxygen (air), Heat; a side drawn full while it is there).
    Take the wood out, put the lid on (it goes out a Wait at a time), take the lid off, fan it. Items: burn more strongly
    (some start under a lid), put it out keeping the wood, put it out with no lid on the bench.
  - **raakh** (s4, s1): Wait burns a piece of the wood or straw; smoke rises and ash falls; Look closely shows the gases in the
    air. Say where each went (Into the air / Left behind), then Done.
  - **jang** (s3): iron nails and railing, each to be rusted or kept bright. Put each in the damp place (air and water) or the
    dry place (air, no water) and tap Another day; rust grows only where it is damp. No flame.

mal-rule acts; nothing is dispatched.
