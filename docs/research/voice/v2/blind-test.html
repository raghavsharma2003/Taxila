Teacher voice: blind listening test

You will hear short clips of a teacher talking to a child, in Hinglish, Hindi and English. Every clip has a random code, and the codes say nothing about where a clip came from. Listen to each clip to the end, then rate it.

How to rate

  • Native Indian: does this sound like someone who grew up speaking here, not a foreigner speaking Hindi? (1 = clearly foreign, 5 = clearly native)
  • Natural: does the speech flow like a person talking, with natural rhythm and pauses? (1 = mechanical, 5 = fully natural)
  • Warmth: would a child feel this teacher is kind and patient with them? (1 = cold, 5 = very warm)
  • Hindi pronunciation: are the Hindi words (and Hindi-accented English words) pronounced the way people here say them? (1 = many errors, 5 = perfect)
  • Real person?: your best guess. Clips come from different sources.

Use headphones in a quiet room. Do not replay a clip many times before rating; first impressions are what we need. Some clips are deliberately poor-quality checks; rate them honestly too.
