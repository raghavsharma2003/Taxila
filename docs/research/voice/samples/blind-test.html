Teacher Voice Blind Test

Short clips of a teacher talking to a child. Each clip has a code; nothing tells you how it was made. Listen and rate how it sounds.

How to do it

  • Use headphones if you can, in a quiet room.
  • Play each clip to the end at least once. The sliders unlock when it starts playing.
  • Rate each line on its own. A clip can be very clear and still not sound like a real teacher.
  • 1 means not at all, 5 means completely. There are no right answers, and some clips will sound alike.
  • Your answers save in this browser as you go. You can stop and come back. When you finish, press Export ratings and send the file.
  • If you can't judge a line (for example Hindi pronunciation, if you don't speak Hindi), press can't judge.
