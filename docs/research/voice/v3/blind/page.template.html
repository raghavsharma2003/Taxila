Teacher voice, round 2: which voice sounds like a real teacher?

Each card is one thing a teacher says to a child, with the moment it happens in. Play every voice on the card (each card has 8, in your own random order). For each one, score how much it sounds like a real teacher talking to me, tick anything that went wrong, and add a note if something stands out. Your notes matter most: write what you hear, in any language. Use headphones. Ratings save automatically under your name.

1 = clearly a machine reading · 3 = could pass, but something is off · 5 = a real teacher talking to me

Each person types their own name before starting. Several people can use the same account or phone: ratings are kept separately under each name.
