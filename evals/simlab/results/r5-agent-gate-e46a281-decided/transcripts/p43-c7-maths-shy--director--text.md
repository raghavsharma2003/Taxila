## Parent summary

Practised multiplying numbers ending in zeros by multiplying non-zero parts and counting zeros.
Rudra gave 3 unaided correct attempts.
Discussed using nearby numbers such as 500 for estimation and noted that this changes the exact answer.
Rudra calculated 500 × 50 = 25,000 and 60 × 700 = 42,000.
For 6,000 × 50, Rudra counted the zeros incorrectly; this was not corrected later in the lesson.

Asha practised the method of multiplying the non-zero parts first and then counting all the zeros. Rudra used the method correctly in several examples and understood that rounding to 500 gives an estimate rather than the exact product. There was one zero-counting mix-up in 6,000 × 50, and it was not corrected before the lesson ended. At home, practise a few examples by writing the zero count from both factors before adding the zeros.
