## Parent summary

Practised checking answers by estimating first.

Kabir practised rounding numbers before adding or subtracting, then comparing the estimate with the exact answer. They worked through place-value confusion in 30,00 versus 3,000 and identified that 3,335 is close to 3,000, not the same. In the final example, they were still unsure whether rounding 1,48,700 to 1,50,000 and 2,52,900 to 2,50,000 gave a reasonable estimate; at home, ask them to round each number and compare the estimate with the exact total using the size of the difference.
