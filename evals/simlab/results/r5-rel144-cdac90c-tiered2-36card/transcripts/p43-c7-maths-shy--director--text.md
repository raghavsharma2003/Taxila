## Parent summary

Practised multiplying numbers ending in zeros.
Rudra used the method of multiplying non-zero parts and then counting total zeros.
They answered 50 × 20 = 1,000, 6,000 × 50 = 300,000, 3,000 × 400 = 1,200,000, and 6,000 × 7 = 42,000.
Three unaided attempts were correct; teach-back was not passed.

Asha practised the zero-counting method with Rudra: multiply the non-zero parts, then attach all the zeros. Rudra showed the method correctly in several examples, including 3,000 × 400 and 6,000 × 7. For practice at home, give two or three similar products and ask them to say the non-zero multiplication and zero count aloud.
