## Parent summary

Practised multiplying numbers ending in zeros by multiplying non-zero parts and counting zeros.

Rudra practised multiplying the non-zero parts first and then adding all the zeros from both factors. They correctly used this method for examples such as 60 × 700 = 42,000 and explained the steps aloud. A mix-up remained when a product such as 500 × 60 was treated as 3,000; this was not corrected by the end of the lesson. At home, practise a few examples by writing the non-zero multiplication and counting the zeros separately.
