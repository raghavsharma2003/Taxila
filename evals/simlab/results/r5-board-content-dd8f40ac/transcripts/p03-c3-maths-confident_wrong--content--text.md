## Parent summary

Practised making 100 with tens numbers and jumps on a number line.
Identified 30 + 70 = 100 in 2 of 3 unaided attempts.
Used bridging through 50 to find 46 + 54 = 100.
Counted jumps from 65 to 100 as 4 jumps.

Vihaan practised finding partners to make 100 and counting jumps on the number line. They used place-value parts and bridging through the next ten, such as 46 to 50, then onward to 100. There was an initial mix-up between the amount added and the number of jumps, which they corrected later by counting 70, 80, 90, 100. At home, ask them to show both the total difference and the jumps for examples such as 72 to 100.
