Rudra gave 42,000 for 60 × 700 and 3,000 for 500 × 60.
They counted zeros in several examples, including 6,000 × 90,000 and 500 × 60.

Teach-back was not passed.

Rudra practised multiplying the non-zero parts first and then counting the zeros. They used this method in several examples, including 60 × 700. In 500 × 60, they gave 3,000 instead of 30,000; this mix-up was not corrected later in the lesson. At home, practise a few examples such as 50 × 20 and 500 × 60, checking that zeros in the final product are not dropped.
