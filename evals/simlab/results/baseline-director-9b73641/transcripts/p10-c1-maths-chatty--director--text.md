## Parent summary

Practised showing numbers 11–20 as one bundle of ten and some loose ones.
Saanvi identified 14 as one ten and four loose ones, with prompting.
They identified 17 as one ten and seven ones.
They sometimes counted the bundle as one when finding the total; this confusion remained at the end.
Teach-back was not passed.

Asha practised the language “one ten and some ones” using bundles and loose sticks. Saanvi correctly described 14 as one ten and four loose sticks and 17 as one ten and seven ones when focusing on the loose ones. They mixed up counting the bundle as one versus counting the ten sticks, and this was not fully corrected by the end. At home, make a bundle of 10 small objects and add loose objects, then ask both “How many ones?” and “How many in all?”
