## Parent summary

Practised making 100 with tens and two-digit numbers.

Vihaan practised finding the missing amount to reach 100 and using jumps on a number line. They used splitting into tens and ones, such as 65 to 70 and then 30 more, to find 35; this method worked in both attempts for 65 + 35. They hesitated at first with some jump questions but later gave correct complements such as 38 + 62 and 30 + 70. At home, practise 5 examples by asking, “How many more to make 100?” and have them explain the jumps.
