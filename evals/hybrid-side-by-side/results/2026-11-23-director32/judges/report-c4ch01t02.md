## E. Verdict

**It does not pass.** It is blocked by:
- the roti contradiction (script data);
- promises at 3 (a walk rule: the Hindi switch line is said where there is no Hindi form);
- clause 14 (script lines).

Listening and register reach 3.5. Promises does not. Under clause 16 the script stays staged.

The three changes that would move it most:
1. Give the roti and slack-string cards their own uneven drawings, and remove "blade" from the script.
2. Say the Hindi switch line only where a Hindi form exists.
3. Make the second help after a miss the card's own other reason. Replace the two two-way "Zoya" cards in skill 1, and stop the points from stating their card's answer.

Would I put this script in front of a child? Not yet. Skill 2 now teaches well. But a child who asks for Hindi gets the same Hinglish back, a child stuck on the tile is told the problem again, and the roti card is asked over a drawing that says the opposite.
